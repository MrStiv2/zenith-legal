Privacy Policy

Updated: 4 November 2026

This policy describes Zenith: Defense Gunner, published by Serhii Fedorenko / CampStudio. Contact: campstudiomail@gmail.com.

Game progress

Your progress, guns, upgrades, currencies, settings and a record of credited purchases are stored in a save file on your device. No CampStudio account or sign-in is required. The game does not upload your save file to a CampStudio server. To protect the save against tampering, it is sealed with a key derived on the device from the device identifier provided by the operating system and the app identifier; this identifier stays on the device and is not sent to CampStudio. Removing the app or clearing its storage may erase local progress and unspent currency. Platform backup services may separately back up app data according to your device settings.

Advertising

The game uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) for interstitial ads and optional rewarded ads. AdMob may collect approximate location derived from IP address, device and advertising identifiers, interactions with ads and the app, and diagnostic information such as crashes and performance. These data may be used for delivering and measuring advertising, personalization where permitted, analytics, fraud prevention and service operation. Precise GPS location is not requested by the game. You can reset or delete your advertising ID and limit ad personalization in your device settings. Google Privacy Policy · Google advertising information.

Purchases

Optional cores packs are sold as consumable in-app purchases processed by Google Play through Unity In-App Purchasing (Google Play Billing). The game receives product identifiers, transaction identifiers and purchase receipts to grant each purchase once and avoid duplicate rewards. CampStudio does not receive your payment card number. Google processes payment and account information under its own privacy policy. Purchase history may be retained by Google even if local game data is deleted.

Other platform services

Optional local reminder notifications (for example, when a daily reward is ready) are scheduled on your device, are not sent through a CampStudio server, and can be disabled in system settings. External links open in your selected browser. The game does not request contacts, microphone, camera, precise location or photo-library access.

Sharing, security and retention

Advertising and store providers receive information directly when their services are used; their processing and retention are governed by their policies. Information may be processed outside your country. Data sent by these services is encrypted in transit. CampStudio does not operate a player-account database for this game or sell save files. Local data remains until removed or overwritten; provider records may be retained for security, financial or legal purposes. No storage or transmission method is completely secure.

Your choices and rights

You can play offline, skip optional rewarded ads, manage notification permissions and control advertising identifiers in your device settings. Removing local app data removes local saves but does not delete store transaction records. Depending on your location, you may have access, correction, deletion, objection or other privacy rights. Contact us at the address above; for data controlled by Google, use Google's privacy controls or contact Google directly.

Children

The game is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the game, contact us. Where a higher age of consent applies, follow local requirements and seek a parent or guardian's guidance.

Changes

We may update this policy when game features or service providers change. The latest version and update date are published here.
